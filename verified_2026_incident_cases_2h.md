# Complete Verified List: 2026 Incident Date Bodycam Cases (`>= 2.0 Hours` Pure BWC/Dashcam + Case Documentation, 0 CCTV)

## Qualification Criteria Applied
1. **2026 Incident Date Only**: Every case listed in **Section 1** occurred between **January 1, 2026 and October 30, 2026** (verified via official agency case numbers `26-...`, OPRA/GRAMA/SB 16 records, or `AXON BODY 3/4` hardware timestamps). All 2022–2025 incidents uploaded in 2026 are excluded.
2. **Minimum Duration (`>= 2.00 Hours` / `>= 7,200 Seconds`)**: Only officer-worn Body-Worn Camera (BWC) and patrol vehicle Dashcam/In-Car Camera (ICC) footage is counted.
3. **Zero CCTV / Static Room Cameras (`0 Seconds` Counted)**: All 3rd-party surveillance, store CCTV, Chicago POD/CTA/OEMC cameras, witness cell phones, and static interrogation/interview room videos are strictly subtracted before checking the `2.00-hour` threshold.
4. **Verifiable Case Documentation**: Every case has official documentation (OPRA/GRAMA/FOIA arrest report, probable cause affidavit, CAD log, court docket, or SB 16 / AB 748 investigative PDF binder).
